Petra van Husen, Sundays I Am Off, 2020

Host

Petra van Husen

Photographer, founder and curator of weissraum. Conceptual and narrative photography.

Her work →About Petra →petravanhusen.de →

It starts with gut feeling: we show artists who fascinate us. Artists who have something to say. Ask them about their work, and they tell you what they want to express and why they make it. We are drawn to photography that moves the medium forward, is outstanding in its craft and touches us.
