Petra van Husen, Sundays I Am Off, 2020

Host

Petra van Husen

Photographer, founder and curator of weissraum. Conceptual and narrative photography.

I work narratively and conceptually. My photographs do not depict the world. Whoever photographs, curates. I arrange, guide the eye and, in doing so, shape how we perceive.

Technical precision is not my goal. I deliberately stay within photographic techniques and still want to go further than the medium usually goes. I am interested in images that arouse curiosity, make you think and, on longer looking, reveal hidden stories and unexpected details.

Many things fascinate me. The silence of a city slowly sinking into the sand. The frantic energy of Grand Central in New York. Intimate portraits of human encounter. Studies of movement that capture the core of a moment. Unlikely encounters in familiar surroundings.

Places where time stands still. Moments just before tipping over.

Each of my works is an invitation to see the familiar with new eyes.

It starts with gut feeling: we show artists who fascinate us. Artists who have something to say. Ask them about their work, and they tell you what they want to express and why they make it. We are drawn to photography that moves the medium forward, is outstanding in its craft and touches us.
