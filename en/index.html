Kunstrausch

View works →View the exhibition →

Anyone who walks from place to place will encounter very different artistic voices.

Kunstrausch brings six art venues in Eltville am Rhein together for one weekend. From 11 to 13 December 2026 they open their doors at the same time, showing photography, painting, drawing and sculpture.

We initiated it together with Daniela Schulz (Atelier Leonhard) and Birgid Helmy. At weissraum we show works by Petra van Husen alongside the sculptures of Birgid Helmy and works by the photographer Detlef Böhmer.

All works on show can be purchased, small and large alike.

Petra van Husen, Kuratorin der Ausstellung
