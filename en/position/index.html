Position

© Michael Leukel

Is that even art, or could I do that myself?

Photography faces a hurdle that other art forms don’t: what you want to photograph has to actually exist; an idea in your head isn’t enough. And that’s precisely why photography is such an exciting medium: through the photographer’s gaze, what already exists is transformed into a new statement. And engaging with that—that’s what fascinates me so much about photography.
