Pressetext

weissraum. Atelier für Fotografie. is a gallery for art photography in Eltville in the Rheingau region. Founded in summer 2025 by photographer and curator Petra van Husen, weissraum shows positions that tell stories and reveal more at second glance than at first: conceptual, narrative, with a distinct signature. Its focus is on the original, on unique pieces and small editions, and increasingly on photography as a craft. Analogue and experimental works in particular only unfold in the original, which is why weissraum shows them in a real exhibition space.

weissraum is a co-founder of the art weekend Kunstrausch and maintains close ties to the South African art scene. In 2026, its first open call, "Im Dickicht", took place: from nearly 200 submissions, a jury selected the works for the group exhibition.
