About weissraum

Fotografie worth a second look.

Rosengasse 15, Eltville am Rhein · © Michael Leukel

weissraum. Atelier für Fotografie

There is so much outstanding photography, and so few places where it is truly shown. That is why weissraum exists.

For us, photography is a fantastic medium and a great art form. At weissraum we show positions that tell stories and reveal more at second glance than at first: conceptual, narrative, with a distinct signature.

Our focus is on the original: unique pieces, one-offs and small editions. And increasingly on photography as a craft.

A second focus is our connection to the South African art scene, with partner galleries and artists on the ground.

weissraum is a gallery for photography as art. For collectors looking for originals, for curators in search of new positions and for artists who take their work seriously.

Petra van Husen

Short bio

Studied in Cologne and Barcelona Worked in corporate finance – Düsseldorf, London, Hamburg, New York Several years abroad in Spain, the USA and South Africa 2016–2019 Studied photography at the Prager Fotoschule in Linz (Austria), focusing on narrative and conceptual photography 2021 Diploma / Master Class, final project Sundays I Am Off since summer 2025 weissraum. Atelier für Fotografie, Eltville am Rhein. weissraum is an active place for contemporary narrative-conceptual photography – with changing exhibitions, workshops and a growing network of artists.

The space

Rosengasse 15, Eltville am Rhein

At weissraum we show art live, in a real place in the Frankfurt area. Here, works can be experienced up close: material, surface, format and their presence in the room. With analogue and experimental photography this matters in a special way, because such works only unfold in the original.

© Michael Leukel

Collaboration

What makes weissraum special is the network it is part of. We are co-founders of Kunstrausch, the art weekend in Eltville. Our partner gallery in South Africa is Ultraviolet in Cape Town.

Curators, galleries and institutions are always welcome, and we look forward to working on projects together.

We are always looking for compelling positions that fit our curatorial concept. You have a series that belongs here? Then we would love to see your work. Looking for portfolio advice? Book a portfolio session with us.
