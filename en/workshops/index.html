Cancellation

Your registration for a workshop at weissraum is binding. Because we deliberately keep our groups small, every place is firmly planned for us — and reserved for you.

If you have to cancel and we cannot fill your place again, we keep the full amount. If we can let someone move up — or if we have to cancel a date ourselves — you get your money back, or we find a new date together.
